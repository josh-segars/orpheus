## 9. AI and Automated Processing

The Services use artificial intelligence to compute parts of the Assessment and to generate the narrative text in your Report and Quick Reference Card. Data derived from your inputs is sent to Anthropic's Claude API at two stages of the analysis. What is sent includes portions of your LinkedIn profile content — your headline, About section, industry, location, employment history, skills, the websites and contact links listed on your profile, and samples of your posts and comments — together with your questionnaire responses and the computed scoring output.

The personal information from your LinkedIn OIDC sign-in — your name, email address, and profile picture — is not included in those prompts. A yes/no indicator of whether your profile has a photo is included; the image itself and its URL are not.

Because your posts and comments are sent as written, they may contain the names of other people you have mentioned or replied to. We do not send your connections list or your recommendations; those files are never read from your archive. If your engagement is concentrated on a small number of posts, the web addresses of those posts are also included as part of the engagement analysis.

We have entered into a commercial agreement with Anthropic that prohibits Anthropic from using your data to train its models and that limits Anthropic's retention of prompt and response data.

The Services do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects within the meaning of GDPR Article 22 or UK GDPR Article 22. The Assessment is a diagnostic; the Quick Reference Card is a list of suggested actions; both are intended to inform your own decisions.
