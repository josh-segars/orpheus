## 7. AI and Automated Processing

Your report and quick reference card are produced by a four-stage analysis pipeline:

1. **Ingestion.** Your uploaded LinkedIn Data Archive and Analytics Export are parsed into structured data using deterministic code. No AI is involved.
2. **Rubric scoring.** Selected portions of your profile and content data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which applies a written rubric to produce sub-dimension ratings. **No personal information from the LinkedIn OIDC sign-in (your name, email address, or profile picture) is included in the prompts sent to Anthropic.**
3. **Deterministic scoring.** Quantitative measures are computed from your archive data and combined with the rubric ratings using a fixed formula. No AI is involved.
4. **Narrative generation.** Structured scoring output, your questionnaire answers, and structured recommendation data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate written narrative text. **No personal information from the LinkedIn OIDC sign-in — your name, email address, or profile picture — is included in those prompts**, with one narrow, derived exception: a yes/no indicator of whether your profile has a profile photo, captured from your sign-in profile at the moment you submit, is included in the analysis. The photo itself, and its URL, are never sent.

We do not make automated decisions about you that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects within the meaning of GDPR Article 22 / UK GDPR Article 22. The assessment is a self-improvement diagnostic; decisions arising from it are yours.

We have entered into a commercial agreement with Anthropic that prohibits Anthropic from using your data to train its models and that limits Anthropic's retention of prompt and response data.

The analytical models and scoring methodology evolve over time. We record the methodology version applied to each assessment, and results generated under different versions may not be directly comparable.

## 11. Information Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. These include:

- Encryption of data in transit using industry-standard TLS;
- Encryption of data at rest at the database layer (provided by Supabase);
- Row-level security policies that restrict database access to your own records;
- Single sign-on through LinkedIn OIDC; we do not store passwords;
- Multi-factor authentication for administrative access to our infrastructure;
- Logging and monitoring of administrative-access events; and
- A documented incident-response process.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify the applicable supervisory authority where and when required by law (for GDPR / UK GDPR, without undue delay and, where feasible, within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach), and we will notify you without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms or where notice is otherwise required by law. If the breach involves data covered by our agreement with LinkedIn, we will additionally notify LinkedIn at security@linkedin.com within twenty-four (24) hours.

No system is perfectly secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 13. Sensitive Personal Information

The CCPA / CPRA, GDPR Article 9, UK GDPR Article 9, and similar laws define certain categories of personal information as "sensitive" — for example, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, health data, biometric data, government-issued identifiers, and precise geolocation.

The Services are not designed to elicit sensitive personal information. However, free-text fields in the questionnaire and certain content fields in the LinkedIn Data Archive (for example, the "Inferences_about_you.csv" file) may incidentally contain information of a sensitive character. Our analysis pipeline never opens that file: only seven files are read from your archive (profile, positions, skills, the rich-media log, posts, comments, and reactions), and files such as Inferences_about_you.csv are checked only for their presence by filename, never their contents. They exist solely inside the raw uploaded archive itself, which is retained and deleted as described in Section 10.

Your upload consent (Section 6) covers this incidental content.

We do not use sensitive personal information for any purpose other than:

- To provide the Service you have requested;
- To detect security incidents and protect against malicious activity; and
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell or share sensitive personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you wish to limit our use of sensitive personal information, contact us at contact@orpheussocial.com.

## 15. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We do not set cookies on the Services. We use only the browser storage necessary to operate them:

**Authentication session** — when you sign in, our authentication provider (Supabase) stores your session in your browser's local storage so that you remain signed in. Signing out removes it.

**Interface preference** — a single flag in local storage recording that you have seen the Welcome screen, so that we do not show it to you again on that device.

**Invitation token** — if you arrive through an invitation link, the invitation token is held briefly in session storage to complete sign-in, and is cleared once you are signed in.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, advertising pixels, social-media tracking scripts, or cross-site tracking. Our analytics (Section 4.2) operate without cookies and without storing any identifier on your device.

You can configure your browser to block cookies and site data. Blocking site data for the Services will prevent you from signing in to the portal.
